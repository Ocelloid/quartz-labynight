Los Angeles, 2025.

The vampires of this city have carved it up down to the last block. You were never part of the deal: you are thin-bloods, the half-castes of the night, and nobody here bothers to count you. A month ago you were UCLA students. Then came the cages in a pawnshop basement, someone else's blood in your throat, and the hum of a jeweler's kiln behind the wall. Three nights ago you escaped.

In a single night four powers of the city will come looking for you, and each will offer you a new life at its own price: a baron, a cult, a preacher, and a community of your own kind. Many doors.
