One-shot: The Beast. Vampire: the Masquerade 5th Ed.

Los Angeles, 2025.

Away from the neon lights of Hollywood's boulevards, just half an hour down a winding road, and all that's left around you are the dark hills and glittering eyes among the trees. Here, in the canyons of Topanga, live those who find the city too small for them: biker gangs, smugglers, wild beasts and the Kindred who rule them all.

These last nights, the hills have been restless. Between songs, the local radio station reads out messages to tourists who drove into the canyons and never came back; neighbors complain about some devilry involving animal carcasses; the streets whisper about a new stimulant that provokes aggression and hunger; and during the day, the Kindred suffer nightmares that not even the sleep of the dead can keep away.

The old legends about monsters were told to scare off outsiders. It seems one of them has stopped being a legend. Try to pick up its trail and find out who is really hunting in the hills.
